# BRAND BOOK & MULTI-MEDIUM CAMPAIGN SPECIFICATION
## AURA | Ether Sculptural Parfum
Tagline: "Sculpted Light. Captured Essence."

---

### 1. BRAND IDENTITY & VISUAL DNA
- **Brand Concept**: AURA represents the intersection of sculptural architecture and olfactory art. Rooted in Scandinavian minimalism, the visual visual strategy focuses on the interplay between raw glass geometry, soft natural light refractions, and tactile material contrasts to evoke weightless luxury.
- **Category**: Luxury Beauty & Fragrance
- **Aesthetic Vibe**: High-end Scandinavian Luxury, Sculptural, Minimalist, Pure Glass & Light
- **Materials & Finishes**: Asymmetrical frosted optical glass, brushed titanium metal, matte charcoal slate, pale champagne liquid
- **Color Palette**: Frosted Quartz (#E6E8E6), Champagne Gold (#E5C158), Matte Charcoal (#282828), Limestone Grey (#D3D3C9)
- **Form Factor & Geometry**: Monolithic asymmetrical prism with hand-carved glass bevels and a flush magnetic titanium cap
- **Emblem / Signature Mark**: Subtle laser-etched geometric AURA monogram on cap top and base glass bevel
